About HOYAO Applied Technologies

Bringing materials and technologies into real-world applications.

Who we are

HOYAO Applied Technologies (和曜應用科技股份有限公司) is a company centered on materials and applied technologies. We focus on how materials and technologies are developed, validated, and brought into real industrial contexts.

From building materials toward applied technologies

HOYAO began with a stronger materials and building focus, and is expanding into technical integration, testing and validation, industrial collaboration, and field application. This is not a departure from materials—it places materials within a fuller application pathway.

How we work

We connect materials development, technical validation, and industrial collaboration to turn technology directions into applications that can be tested, introduced, and advanced. We emphasize development, testing, validation, application, integration, technical support, and industry collaboration—not a product-catalog identity.

The role of our Nantou base

Nantou is an important development base for HOYAO at this stage—connecting local material resources, research and validation, industrial collaboration, and real application settings. It is part of the company story, not the company’s entire market boundary.

Collaboration we welcome

We welcome discussions on materials, systems, validation, application development, and emerging-mobility related collaboration.
