Incorporation and early development

    Related details →
  2. 06

    Joined the Zhongxing New Village incubator

    Selected as a physical resident team in the National Development Council’s Cohort 6 regional revitalization incubator in Nantou.

    Related details →
  3. 07.26

    Related details →

    Related details →
  6. 09.03

    Nantou County Local SBIR approval

    Included in the Year 115 (2026) Local Industry Innovation R&D Promotion Program approval list released by Nantou County Government.

    Related details →
  7. 10.02

  8. 10.28

    Related details →
  9. 11.11
