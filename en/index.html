Company

Connecting development, validation, and industry

HOYAO connects materials development, technical validation, and industrial collaboration to convert technology directions into applications that can be tested, introduced, and developed continuously—across bio-based and sustainable materials, fire safety and building applications, and advanced materials and emerging mobility.

Nantou, Taiwan

HOYAO is based at the Zhongxing New Village Regional Revitalization Startup Incubator and uses Nantou as an important development base at this stage—connecting local material resources, technical validation, industry collaboration, and real-world application settings.
