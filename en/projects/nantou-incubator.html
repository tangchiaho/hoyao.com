Selected for the 6th Cohort of the Zhongxing New Village Regional Revitalization Startup Incubator

HOYAO Applied Technologies (和曜應用科技股份有限公司) was selected as a physical resident team in the 6th cohort and is based in Zhongxing New Village, Nantou.

HOYAO has been selected as a physical resident team in the 6th cohort of the Zhongxing New Village Regional Revitalization Startup Incubator and is based in Zhongxing New Village, Nantou. The incubator was established by Taiwan’s National Development Council and is operated by the Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI). Through residency space, professional mentoring, and industry resources, it supports teams in turning innovative ideas into practical action.

This residency is an important step in HOYAO’s development of applied technologies and industry collaboration. Going forward, HOYAO will continue to use Nantou as an important base at this stage to advance material applications, technical validation, industry collaboration, and real-world field connections.

The residency application was submitted under the name 和曜建材科技股份有限公司. The company has since completed its registered name change to 和曜應用科技股份有限公司 / HOYAO Applied Technologies.
