HOYAO Selected for NDC Zhongxing New Village Regional Revitalization Incubator Cohort 6 Physical Residency

HOYAO was selected for the National Development Council Zhongxing New Village Regional Revitalization Startup Incubator Cohort 6 physical residency (2026, Nantou).

HOYAO was selected as a physical resident team in Cohort 6 of the Zhongxing New Village Regional Revitalization Startup Incubator and is based in Zhongxing New Village, Nantou. The incubator was established by Taiwan’s National Development Council and is operated by the Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI). Through residency space, professional mentoring, and industry resources, it supports teams in turning innovative ideas into practical action.

This residency is an important step in HOYAO’s development of applied technologies and industry collaboration. Going forward, HOYAO will continue to use Nantou as an important base at this stage to advance material applications, technical validation, industry collaboration, and real-world field connections.
