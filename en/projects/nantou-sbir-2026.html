HOYAO｜Nantou County 2026 Local SBIR Approval

HOYAO is listed among firms approved in Nantou County’s announced 2026 Local SBIR results.

Notice summary

The program is the “Local Industry Innovation R&D Promotion Program” (Local SBIR) for 2026 (ROC year 115). On 3 September 2026, the Construction Department of Nantou County Government announced the review results: 26 applications received, 16 firms approved (62% approval rate). HOYAO is included on the approved list.

Local SBIR supports innovation and R&D among SMEs in Nantou. HOYAO’s work centers on materials and applied technologies, advancing verifiable research and industry collaboration from its Nantou base.

Company-name mapping

The county notice lists 和曜建材科技股份有限公司. The registered name was formally changed to 和曜應用科技股份有限公司 on 12 August 2026. See: company name change.
