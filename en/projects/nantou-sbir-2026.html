Nantou County Year 115 (2026) Local SBIR｜HOYAO Project Record

This page records HOYAO’s participation in Nantou County’s Local Industry Innovation R&D Promotion Program (Local SBIR).

Program context

The formal program name is the Local Industry Innovation R&D Promotion Program (Local SBIR), for Year 115 (2026), administered by Nantou County Government. The program encourages innovative R&D by SMEs in the county. HOYAO continues to focus on materials and application technologies in Nantou through verifiable research and industry collaboration.

Approval summary

On 3 September 2026, the Construction Department of Nantou County Government published review results: 26 applications received, 16 firms approved. HOYAO is on the approved list. Approval refers to plan selection and does not mean grant funds have been fully disbursed. For the announcement update, see: Updates: HOYAO Receives Nantou County Year 115 (2026) Local SBIR Plan Approval.

Name mapping

The county notice lists 和曜建材科技股份有限公司. The company name was formally changed to 和曜應用科技股份有限公司 on 12 August 2026. See: company name change.
