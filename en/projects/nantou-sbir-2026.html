HOYAO｜Nantou County 2026 Local SBIR R&D Project

HOYAO was approved under Nantou County Government’s Year 115 (2026) Local Industry Innovation R&D Promotion Program (Local SBIR), supporting further R&D and industrial collaboration in materials applications.

About the program

The formal program name is the Local Industry Innovation R&D Promotion Program (Local SBIR), for Year 115 (2026), administered by Nantou County Government. The program encourages innovative R&D by SMEs in the county. HOYAO continues to focus on materials and application technologies in Nantou through verifiable research and industry collaboration.

Approval results

On 3 September 2026, the Construction Department of Nantou County Government published review results: 26 applications received, 16 firms approved. HOYAO is on the approved list. For the official results, see HOYAO’s Local SBIR approval announcement.

Company name

The county notice lists 和曜建材科技股份有限公司. The company name was formally changed to 和曜應用科技股份有限公司 on 12 August 2026. See: company name change.
