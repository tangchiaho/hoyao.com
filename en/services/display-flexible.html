Display & Flexible Spaces

Display & flexible spaces

For brand pop-ups, exhibitions, retail display, and short-term spaces, HOYAO reviews display and storage that can be taken apart, adjusted, and used again.

This extends materials supply, cabinetry, and woodworking to jobs with a limited run, where the pieces need to come apart or be used again.

What to send first

Include the venue, how it will be used, the expected date, the dimensions, and the budget. Plans or reference photos help. If the same job also needs panels, cabinetry, or fixed woodworking, see Materials & Panel Supply, Cabinetry & Custom Furniture, and Woodworking & Spatial Integration.
