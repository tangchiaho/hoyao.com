HOYAO Materials & Panels

Materials & panel supply

Board for cabinets, furniture, and interiors, plus bamboo and plant-fiber materials. The application comes first; the specification follows the job.

01

Cabinetry & furniture panels

Cabinetry & Furniture Panels

Board suited to system cabinets, storage furniture, display cases, office furniture, and other furniture making.

Where it is used

Carcasses, shelves, doors, and built-in furniture. The useful questions are stability, how the edge and face will be finished, and whether hinges and runners can be fitted.

Structural board can be discussed as MDF, particleboard, or plywood when the job needs that distinction. Thickness, grade, and face are confirmed per inquiry.

Settings

Residential system cabinets, retail and exhibition display, office storage, and furniture parts that have to meet a fixed size.

02

Decorative & interior panels

Decorative & Interior Panels

Faces, wood grains, and colors for furniture, interior fit-out, and commercial space.

Where it is used

Doors, tops, wall surfaces, and any face that should read as continuous in a room. The match is between the decorative face, the cabinet substrate, and nearby woodwork.

Settings

Visible surfaces in a home, reception and offices, and retail or brand spaces that need one consistent finish.

03

Bamboo & plant-fiber materials

Bamboo & Plant-Fiber Materials

Bamboo, plant fiber, and agroforestry materials for furniture parts and surfaces.

Supply and development

This item is supply: whether bamboo, plant fiber, and related faces fit a furniture or interior job. Development, testing, and application research sit separately, under Bio-based & Sustainable Materials.

Settings

Furniture, surfaces, and spatial elements where bamboo or plant fiber is part of the brief. Specifications are confirmed per job.

How supply works

HOYAO replies with a material that fits, the fabrication conditions, and timing.
