Cabinetry & Custom Furniture

Cabinetry & custom furniture

More flexibility in how storage and furniture are used.

From system cabinets, display cases, and storage shelving to modular furniture, planning and making follow the room, how it is used, and the material.

For homes, offices, commercial display, and storage with a specific job to do, balancing how it looks, how it works, and room to adjust it later.

What to send first

A drawing is useful, not required. A note on the room size, what has to be stored, and the look you want is enough to start. Plans or photos make the dimensions easier to read.

Materials and making

Panels can be chosen at the same time. See HOYAO Materials & Panels. Once the design direction is clear, fabrication follows: face, hardware, and the clearance the site needs for installation.

Ask about cabinetry

Include the space, the dimensions, and the design direction.
