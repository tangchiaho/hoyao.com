Cabinetry & Custom Furniture

Cabinetry & custom furniture

System cabinets, storage, custom furniture, display cases, and office furniture, planned for how a home or a commercial room is actually used. Materials, design, and making belong in one inquiry.

What this covers

  • System cabinets and storage for kitchens, clothing, and everyday use.
  • Custom furniture: tables, cabinets, and built-ins made to a size and a way of using them.
  • Display cases and office furniture for retail, reception, and workspaces.
  • Wall, tall, and built-in cabinets for homes and commercial rooms.

What to send first

A drawing is useful, not required. A note on the room size, what has to be stored, and the look you want is enough to start. Plans or photos make the dimensions easier to read.

Materials and making

Panels can be chosen at the same time. See HOYAO Materials & Panels. Once the design direction is clear, fabrication follows: face, hardware, and the clearance the site needs for installation.

Ask about cabinetry

Include the space, the dimensions, and the design direction.
