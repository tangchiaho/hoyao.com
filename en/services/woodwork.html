Woodworking & Spatial Integration

Woodworking & spatial integration

Start from the space, then settle how it should be made.

For homes and commercial interiors, HOYAO reviews woodworking that stays with the space.

The scale and the site determine how materials, design, fabrication, and specialist installation come together. Site work is carried out by the partner team.

What to send first

Include the location and the scope, with plans, elevations, or site photos. If the same job also needs panels or cabinetry, see Materials & Panel Supply and Cabinetry & Custom Furniture. For a limited run that needs to be taken apart or used again, see Display & Flexible Spaces.

Ask about woodworking

Include the location, the scope of work, and any drawings.
