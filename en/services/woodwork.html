Woodworking & Project Coordination

Woodworking & project coordination

Interior woodwork, cabinet fit-out, display and commercial spaces, and furniture fabrication can be reviewed together with the materials, the drawings, and the site.

What can be taken on

  • Interior woodwork: wall surfaces, built-ins, and woodwork that joins the cabinetry.
  • Cabinet fit-out: system or custom cabinets that have to be installed on site.
  • Display and commercial rooms: retail, reception, and brand spaces.
  • Furniture and woodwork fabrication from drawings or a sample.
  • Joining materials to the job: how panels, faces, and site conditions meet.

How a project starts

Email the location and the scope, and attach plans, elevations, or site photos. HOYAO uses that to bring panel supply, designers, woodworkers, and specialist partners into one review, then moves the work forward. Site work is carried out by the partner team.

If the same job also needs panels or cabinetry, see Materials & Panel Supply and Cabinetry & Custom Furniture.

Ask about a woodworking project

Include the location, the scope of work, and any drawings.
