HOYAO Founded

HOYAO was founded on 29 April 2026. At incorporation, the registered company name was 和曜建材科技股份有限公司.

Since founding, HOYAO has focused on materials and application technologies—advancing materials applications, technical validation, and industrial collaboration.

On 12 August 2026, the registered company name was formally changed to 和曜應用科技股份有限公司. The public brand remains HOYAO Applied Technologies / HOYAO; the current legal registered name is 和曜應用科技股份有限公司.
