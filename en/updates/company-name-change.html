Update · 2026.08.12

Company name formally changed to HOYAO Applied Technologies

On 12 August 2026, HOYAO’s registered company name was formally approved to change from 和曜建材科技股份有限公司 to 和曜應用科技股份有限公司.

This change reflects HOYAO’s expansion from a building-materials starting point toward technical integration, validation, and industrial application. The public brand remains HOYAO Applied Technologies / HOYAO; the current registered legal name is 和曜應用科技股份有限公司.

The former name 和曜建材科技股份有限公司 remains available for search and historical document reference.
