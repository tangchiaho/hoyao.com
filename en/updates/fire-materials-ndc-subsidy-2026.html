HOYAO Fire Materials Application Project Receives NDC Subsidy Approval

HOYAO has advanced fire materials application development, completed this stage of trial production and preliminary testing, and received National Development Council (NDC) subsidy approval.

UpdatesFire materialsNDC

On 10 November 2026, the National Development Council published the Year 115 (2026) subsidy list for resident teams of the Zhongxing New Village Regional Revitalization Startup Incubator. HOYAO Applied Technologies received approved subsidy support for fire materials application development.

HOYAO has completed this stage of material trial production and preliminary testing. Related samples and test records will support later evaluation of product application feasibility.

Related work stream: Fire Safety & Building Applications. Incubator residency: Zhongxing New Village incubator residency.
