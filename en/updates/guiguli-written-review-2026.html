HOYAO Passes Written Review for the 2026 Return-to-Hometown Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition

HOYAO is listed among 60 nationwide teams that passed written review for the Administration for Digital Industries’ 2026 Return-to-Hometown Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition.

UpdatesReturn-to-HometownNantou

HOYAO Applied Technologies Co., Ltd. participated in the “2026 Return-to-Hometown Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition” organized by the Administration for Digital Industries, Ministry of Digital Affairs, with Nantou County as its competition venue. The company has formally passed written review and is listed among 60 nationwide teams that passed this stage.

HOYAO will next take part in the preliminary proposal selection, continuing to advance corporate development and local industry collaboration.
