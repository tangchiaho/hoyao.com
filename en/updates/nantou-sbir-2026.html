Update · 2026.10.13

Selected for Nantou County’s 2026 Local SBIR program

HOYAO is listed among firms approved in Nantou County’s announced 2026 “Local Industry Innovation R&D Promotion Program” (Local SBIR) results.

On 3 September 2026, the Construction Department of Nantou County Government published the 2026 Local SBIR review results. The notice states that 26 applications were received and 16 firms were approved (62% approval rate). The published list includes HOYAO under the former name 和曜建材科技股份有限公司.

HOYAO’s registered company name was formally changed to 和曜應用科技股份有限公司 on 12 August 2026. See: Company name formally changed to HOYAO Applied Technologies.

This page states only what the official announcement confirms. It does not disclose an individual project title, grant amount, or execution details, and it does not claim project acceptance or certification outcomes.

Project record: Nantou County 2026 Local SBIR.
