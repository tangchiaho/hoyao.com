HOYAO Selected for Nantou County's 2026 Local SBIR Program

HOYAO is listed among firms approved under Nantou County’s 2026 Local Industry Innovation R&D Promotion Program (Local SBIR).

UpdatesLocal SBIRNantou

On 3 September 2026, the Construction Department of Nantou County Government published the 2026 Local SBIR review results. The notice states that 26 applications were received during the year and 16 firms were approved (62% pass rate). HOYAO is included on the approved list; the notice records the former company name “和曜建材科技股份有限公司”.

The company name was formally changed to “和曜應用科技股份有限公司” on 12 August 2026. See: Company name formally changed to HOYAO Applied Technologies.

Local SBIR supports innovative R&D by SMEs in the county. HOYAO continues to focus on materials and application technologies in Nantou through verifiable research and industry collaboration.

Project note: Nantou County 2026 Local SBIR.
