HOYAO Visits Zhushan Culture Park to Explore Bamboo Materials and Collaborative Applications

HOYAO visited Zhushan Culture Park in Nantou to exchange on bamboo culture, local industry, and site applications, and signed a memorandum of understanding with the park.

UpdatesSite exchangeIndustry collaborationBio-based & sustainable materials

Field exchange｜Zhushan Culture Park

The HOYAO team visited Zhushan Culture Park (Bamboo Music & Family Interactive Center) to discuss park development, cultural exhibition, site operations, and potential applications of bamboo materials within the local industry.

Zhushan Culture Park presents bamboo across ecology, traditional craft, furniture, everyday objects, and spatial design. The visit helped HOYAO better understand the development context, cultural value, and practical site needs of the local bamboo industry—and to explore, from a materials and applied-technology perspective, how bamboo can enter products, buildings, and public settings.

During the exchange, both sides signed a memorandum of understanding as a foundation for further dialogue and collaboration. Going forward, HOYAO will continue to assess feasible directions for connecting bamboo materials with local sites through materials applications, technical validation, and industry partnership.

For HOYAO, engaging different industries and sites is not only about visiting a space—it is a process of understanding local experience, real needs, and development context. These on-site exchanges will inform subsequent materials development and application planning.
