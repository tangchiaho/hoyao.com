Zhushan Kai Fan Le Wins Third Place in Nantou's 2026 Playful Space Competition

On 2 October 2026, the Nantou County Youth Development Office announced the final results; the work proposed and creatively directed by Tang Chia-Ho received third place.

On 2 October 2026, the Nantou County Youth Development Office announced the final results of the 2026 “玩轉空間” environmental design competition.

Zhushan Kai Fan Le – A Sustainable Garden of Reborn Bamboo, proposed and creatively directed by Tang Chia-Ho, received third place.

The installation brings together bamboo, plant-based and circular materials, local culture, and participatory placemaking to explore material regeneration and the revitalization of public spaces in Zhushan, Nantou.

Tang Chia-Ho served as the proposer, creative lead, and award recipient. Project execution involved Tang Chia-Ho and Chen Pin-Yi, with materials collaboration from HOYAO APPLIED TECHNOLOGIES and 新竹金 BAMBOO.

The work had previously been shortlisted in the competition announced on 22 July 2026.

Visitors can explore the official project page to learn more about the concept, materials, development process, and participatory experience: Zhushan Kai Fan Le.
