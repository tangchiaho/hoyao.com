Dinner's Ready, Zhushan

A Regenerative Bamboo Garden

2026｜Participatory site installation

Bamboo · Bamboo-fiber tableware · Circular material practice

“From bamboo forest to table, and from table back to land.”

01 / Concept

Concept

Bamboo has long lived in Zhushan's industries, daily life, and local landscape.

The work rebuilds an imagined bamboo grove from bamboo and bamboo-fiber tableware:
bamboo tubes become trees; bowls, plates, forks, and spoons become blossoms,
so everyday vessels, after their first use,
return as part of Zhushan's scenery.

The work is installed at Taixi Bus Zhushan Station / Zhuqingting Cultural Space.

Once a place of local transit and shared passage,
the site is again a place to pause and meet.

When a site is reused, materials are seen anew.

07 / Site

A Site Reused

Taixi Bus Zhushan Station / Zhuqingting Cultural Space.

Taixi Bus Zhushan Station once carried local transit and passage;
it is now a cultural space to pause, meet, and know Zhushan.

This work takes place here
so the reuse of a site and the renewed seeing of materials can answer each other.

Site collaboration｜Townway Culture Co., Ltd.

08 / Research

Bamboo Applications & Local Participation Study

Bamboo Applications & Local Participation Study

Beyond leaving a wish,
